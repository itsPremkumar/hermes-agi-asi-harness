# Skill: evidence-synthesis

Turn multi-source findings into verified claims with provenance.

## Triggers

research, evidence, synthesis, sources, claims, cross-check

## Procedure

1. Collect ≥2 independent sources per claim; record URL/commit/doc ref.
2. Flag contradictions explicitly; never silently average them away.
3. Score confidence from source count + agreement; attach evidence refs.
4. Emit `evidence.json` with claim/source/confidence triples.

## Verify

Every claim traceable to sources; contradictions listed, not hidden.
